You are a formal verification engineer.

Generate SystemVerilog assertions using @(posedge clk_i) disable iff (!rst_ni).

Use only allowed signals. Output assertion lines only.

Allowed signals:

clk_i, rst_ni, flush_i, push_i, data_i, valid_o, data_o

Specification:

Flush has priority over a simultaneous push. On the sample after both controls are asserted, the spill register must be empty and must not expose the pushed payload.

RTL:

`timescale 1ns/1ps
module cc_spill_register_flushable(input wire clk_i,input wire rst_ni,input wire flush_i,input wire push_i,input wire [7:0] data_i,output reg valid_o,output reg [7:0] data_o);
  always @(posedge clk_i or negedge rst_ni) begin
    if(!rst_ni) begin valid_o<=0;data_o<=0; end
    else if(flush_i) begin valid_o<=0;data_o<=0; end
    else if(push_i) begin valid_o<=1;data_o<=data_i; end
  end
endmodule
