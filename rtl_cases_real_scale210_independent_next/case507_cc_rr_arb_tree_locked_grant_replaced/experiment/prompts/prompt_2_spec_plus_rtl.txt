Generate SystemVerilog assertions using @(posedge clk_i) disable iff (!rst_ni).

Use only allowed signals. Output assertion lines only.

Allowed signals:

clk_i, rst_ni, req_i, ready_i, gnt_o

Specification:

After a requester is granted while the consumer is not ready, the arbiter must retain that grant until the transfer completes, even if another request appears and the original request changes.

RTL:

`timescale 1ns/1ps
module cc_rr_arb_tree(input wire clk_i,input wire rst_ni,input wire [1:0] req_i,input wire ready_i,output reg [1:0] gnt_o);
  reg locked;
  always @(posedge clk_i or negedge rst_ni) begin
    if(!rst_ni) begin gnt_o<=0;locked<=0; end
    else if(locked) begin if(ready_i) begin locked<=0;gnt_o<=0;end end
    else if(req_i[0]) begin gnt_o<=2'b01;locked<=!ready_i;end
    else if(req_i[1]) begin gnt_o<=2'b10;locked<=!ready_i;end else gnt_o<=0;
  end
endmodule
